BUILD lights the sections one after another and keeps them on; STEPS shows one at a time. Both finish on an all-glow step that holds twice as long, then start over — so 10 sections play as 10 pieces + all.
Sections are contiguous, so editing one end moves the seam it shares with its neighbour. LIGHT previews a section on the real board.
